## 🎯 USER FLOW

```
1. User opens dashboard
   ↓
2. Views trending hashtags
   ↓
3. Selects region (optional)
   ↓
4. Clicks on trend
   ↓
5. Clicks "Suggest Content"
   ↓
6. AI generates suggestions
   ↓
7. Edits tweet content
   ↓
8. Adds/removes hashtags
   ↓
9. Chooses action:

   ↓
10. Tweet appears in Activity panel
   ↓
11. View engagement metrics
   ↓
12. Manage/delete as needed
```
