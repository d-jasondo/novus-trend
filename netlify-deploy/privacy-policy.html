Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 25, 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to Novus Trend. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you about how we handle your personal data when you use our social media management platform and tell you about your privacy rights.

2. Information We Collect

We collect and process the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile information from connected social media accounts (LinkedIn, Twitter/X)
  • Authentication Data: OAuth tokens and credentials for social media platform integration
  • Content Data: Posts, media files, captions, and scheduling information you create through our platform
  • Usage Data: Information about how you use our service, including features accessed and actions performed
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and analytics data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain our social media management services
  • Authenticate and connect your social media accounts
  • Post content to your connected social media platforms on your behalf
  • Generate AI-powered content recommendations and captions
  • Analyze trending topics and hashtags for your content strategy
  • Improve our services and develop new features
  • Communicate with you about service updates and support
  • Ensure the security and integrity of our platform

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

  • LinkedIn API: To authenticate users and post content to LinkedIn
  • Twitter/X API: To authenticate users and post content to Twitter/X
  • Google Gemini AI: To generate AI-powered content and captions
  • RapidAPI: To fetch trending topics and hashtags

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We only share data with these services as necessary to provide our core functionality, and each service has its own privacy policy governing their use of your data.

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your OAuth tokens are stored securely, and we use industry-standard encryption protocols.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data we hold
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Revoke access to your connected social media accounts at any time
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to processing of your personal data

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this privacy policy. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

8. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to maintain user sessions, remember preferences, and analyze platform usage. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.

9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.
